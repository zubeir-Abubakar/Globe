# Licensing and third-party assets

This document inventories the licensing position of the Globe Harvest source
release. It is an engineering and commercial-preparation review, not legal advice.
The seller and buyer should have the final transfer agreement reviewed by a
qualified lawyer in the relevant jurisdiction.

## Project source code

The application code in this repository is presented as original project work.
Repository evidence does not show copied application code from another product.
That is a statement about the repository, not a legal warranty of authorship or
chain of title.

**No open-source licence has been selected.** There is no `LICENSE` file, and
`package.json` remains `"private": true`. Do not describe the code as MIT,
Apache-2.0, open source or freely redistributable. The exclusive sale must be
governed by a separate written assignment or licence agreement. Do not add an
open-source licence as part of this release.

## Brand artwork and name

The master file `public/brand/logo2.svg` was supplied for the Globe Harvest brand.
It contains no embedded author, copyright, source or licence metadata. Git history
shows that it was added in the initial project commit (`0681fd2`), but that does not
establish who created it or whether the seller owns it.

The other files in `public/brand` are derived lockups, emblem variants, a favicon
and raster app icons. Their provenance depends on the master artwork.

**Ownership and trademark status are unresolved.** Before an exclusive transfer,
the seller must choose one of these paths:

1. Confirm in writing that the seller created the artwork or owns the necessary
   rights, and assign those rights in the sale agreement.
2. Commission a replacement logo and regenerate the derived lockups/icons.
3. Exclude the brand artwork from the sale and let the buyer supply its own brand.

Do not claim that the logo or the Globe Harvest name is cleared, registered,
exclusive or free of third-party rights until that has been independently
confirmed. The logo was not silently replaced in this release.

## Photography

All 17 photographs in `public/photos` are CC0 or public domain. The complete
author, title, source, licence and modification record is in `PHOTO-CREDITS.md`
and `public/photos/credits.json`.

The three previous CC BY-SA photographs were replaced with CC0 alternatives:

| File | Replacement | Author | Licence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `mangoes.jpg` | Carabao Mangoes from Cebu, Philippines | Bim24 | CC0 1.0 |
| `tropical.jpg` | Tropical fruit bowl window (Unsplash) | Marcello Aquino | CC0 1.0 |
| `feat-hass.jpg` | Nice Avocados | ProjectManhattan | CC0 1.0 |

CC0 and public-domain works can be used commercially and modified without a
share-alike obligation. Attribution is retained for transparency. **They are not
exclusive assets:** other parties may use the same source images. The buyer can
include the local copies in a proprietary website package, but the seller cannot
promise exclusive ownership of the underlying photograph.

No CC BY-SA, CC BY-NC, "unknown source" or unlicensed stock image remains in the
release snapshot.

## Generated artwork

The catalogue and editorial SVGs in `public/art` are generated by
`scripts/generate-art.mjs` from geometric primitives defined in that script. They
are original project artwork, not downloaded stock. Their licensing follows the
project's yet-to-be-decided source terms.

`scripts/generate-art.mjs` writes only `public/art`; it deliberately does not
overwrite the supplied brand files.

## Fonts

The site loads **DM Serif Display** and **Inter** from Google Fonts through the CSS
import in `src/app/tokens.css`. Both families are distributed under the SIL Open
Font License 1.1 (OFL-1.1). Font binaries are not stored in this repository. If the
buyer self-hosts the fonts, keep the OFL notices with the font files.

## Third-party services

No paid account, API key, analytics account, database, CMS or email-service
credential is included. The buyer must create and own any hosting, domain, form
delivery or other service account used in production. The recommended deployment
does not require a database.

## Exclusivity and transfer flags

The following points require an explicit owner decision before an exclusive sale:

1. **Source-code terms.** The code has no licence or signed assignment yet. The
   sale agreement must transfer or license the code on stated terms.
2. **Brand ownership.** The supplied master logo's authorship, copyright and
   trademark status are unverified. Confirm ownership, replace it, or exclude it.
3. **Photography is non-exclusive.** All photos are CC0/public domain. They can be
   included in the package, but the seller cannot promise exclusive rights to the
   images themselves.
4. **LGPL and attribution notices.** The locked image pipeline includes
   LGPL-licensed libvips binaries, and `caniuse-lite` is CC-BY-4.0. Distribution
   and notice obligations must be met.

Resolve or expressly accept these points in the sale agreement. Do not claim a
licence, ownership or clearance that has not been documented.
