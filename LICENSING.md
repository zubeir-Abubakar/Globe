# Licensing and third-party assets

This document records the licensing position of the Globe Harvest source release.
It is an engineering inventory, not legal advice. The owner should obtain legal
review before selling the project or publishing it as an open repository.

## Project code

**No licence has been selected for the application source code.** There is no
`LICENSE` file at the repository root, and `package.json` is marked `"private": true`.
Do not assume that the code may be copied, modified or redistributed under an
open-source licence. The owner must decide the sale or licence terms explicitly.

Do not add MIT, Apache-2.0 or another licence by default; that decision affects the
commercial exclusivity of the product.

## Brand artwork

The master file `public/brand/logo2.svg` was supplied for the Globe Harvest brand.
The other files in `public/brand` are derived lockups, emblem variants, a favicon/app
icon and raster app icons. Provenance and ownership have not been independently
verified in this repository. Before a public or commercial release, the owner should
confirm that they hold the necessary rights and complete a trademark/name search.
Do not describe the supplied brand artwork as third-party stock or as an asset whose
rights have already been cleared.

The files are distributed only as part of this project's source package.

## Generated artwork

The catalogue and editorial SVGs in `public/art` are generated by
`scripts/generate-art.mjs` from geometric primitives defined in that script. They are
original project artwork, not downloaded stock. Their licensing follows the project's
yet-to-be-decided source terms.

`scripts/generate-art.mjs` writes only `public/art`; it deliberately does not
overwrite the supplied brand files.

## Photography

The photographs in `public/photos` are third-party Wikimedia Commons images. The
complete author, title, source and licence record is in `PHOTO-CREDITS.md` and
`public/photos/credits.json`. Keep both files with the photos.

| Licence | Files | Obligations |
| --- | --- | --- |
| CC0 1.0 | Most homepage and featured photography | No attribution required; credit is retained for transparency. |
| CC BY-SA 2.5 | `tropical.jpg` (papaya) | Credit the author, link the licence, indicate modifications and license adaptations under CC BY-SA 2.5. |
| CC BY-SA 3.0 | `mangoes.jpg` | Credit the author, link the licence, indicate modifications and license adaptations under CC BY-SA 3.0 or later. |
| CC BY-SA 4.0 | `feat-hass.jpg` | Credit the author, link the licence, indicate modifications and license adaptations under CC BY-SA 4.0. |

The CC BY-SA photographs can be used on a commercial website, but they cannot be
licensed to a buyer on an exclusive basis. If the product sale requires exclusive
rights to every asset, replace those images with commissioned or separately licensed
photography and update both credits files.

`grapes.jpg` is cropped from a CC0 original; the credit records the modification.

## Fonts

The site loads **DM Serif Display** and **Inter** from Google Fonts via the CSS
import in `src/app/tokens.css`. Both families are distributed under the SIL Open Font
License 1.1 (OFL-1.1). The font binaries are not stored in this repository. If the
site is moved to a context where Google Fonts is unavailable, self-host the OFL font
files and keep their licence notices with them.

buyer receives a distributed binary or container, include the applicable licence
notices and review the LGPL obligations. This is an engineering flag, not legal
advice.

Review the lockfile and generated dependency notices again before a commercial
sale, because later updates can change the inventory.

## Exclusivity flag

Three issues need an owner decision before public release or exclusive sale:

1. **The application code has no licence or assignment document.**
2. **The supplied brand artwork's ownership and trademark status are unverified.**
3. **CC BY-SA photography carries share-alike obligations and is not exclusive.**
4. **The locked runtime image pipeline includes LGPL-licensed libvips binaries, and
   browser-support data is CC-BY-4.0.** These do not prevent a commercial website,
   but distribution and attribution obligations must be met.

Resolve or explicitly accept these points in the sale agreement. Do not claim a
licence, ownership or clearance that has not been documented.
