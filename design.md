# Globe Harvest — design system

A premium agricultural trading identity: deep forest green, warm ivory, harvest gold
and editorial serif display type. The reference point is a specialist produce trading
house, not a grocery retailer.

## Brand

- Name: **Globe Harvest**
- Positioning: premium fresh-produce import, export and wholesale
- Tone: precise, calm, international, commercially credible
- Promise language is capability-based; no invented certifications, volumes, years,
  customers, facilities or supplier relationships.

The supplied master artwork and its release derivatives live in `public/brand/`:

- `logo2.svg` — supplied master artwork; preserve this source file
- `logo.svg` — cropped stacked lockup for light surfaces
- `logo-inverse.svg` — stacked lockup for dark surfaces
- `logo-horizontal.svg` — horizontal lockup for the site header
- `logo-horizontal-inverse.svg` — horizontal lockup for the dark footer
- `logo-mark.svg` — standalone emblem
- `icon.svg` — app icon and favicon
- `icon-192.png`, `icon-512.png`, `apple-touch-icon.png` — raster app icons

The supplied emblem and wordmark are rendered in deep green (`#0d4541`). Keep their
proportions; do not recolour, redraw or stretch the artwork when replacing a file.

Avoid saturated supermarket greens, gradient buttons and multi-colour badges.

## Typography

- Display: **DM Serif Display**, upright, tight tracking, used for h1–h3.
- Interface and body: **Inter**, 400/500/600/700.
- Baseline 16px, body line-height 1.65, prose 1.75.
- Kickers are uppercase Inter at 12px with 0.16em tracking in gold.

## Layout

- Maximum content width 1240px; 1.5rem gutters, 0.875rem on small screens.
- Generous vertical rhythm: sections use `clamp(4rem, 8vw, 6.5rem)`.
- Asymmetric two-column editorial layouts; no centred-everything template grids.
- Cards use 1px warm borders and a soft green-tinted shadow, not heavy elevation.

## Components

- Sticky glass navigation with a pill container and a product mega-menu.
- Accessible mobile drawer with scrim and scroll lock.
- Search dialog with result kinds (category, product, page, market note).
- Category grid of five columns, two on mobile.
- Product cards: 1.3 aspect artwork, category label, sourcing-region chips.
- Product detail: gallery with two original artworks and a specification CTA.
- Contact form: two-column fields, inline errors, explicit demonstration notice.
- Footer: brand lockup, demonstration disclaimer, three link columns, contact block.

## Motion

- Brief entrance and hover transitions only (160–280ms, `cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1)`).
- Headings use the existing line-mask reveal; artwork has one soft entrance.
- `prefers-reduced-motion` removes reveal transforms and animations.

## Imagery

Catalogue and editorial artwork in `public/art` is SVG generated by
`scripts/generate-art.mjs`: flat produce plates with a shared background language
(soft arc, thin ring, field curve). The brand artwork in `public/brand` is supplied
separately and is not generated by that script.

Homepage photography in `public/photos` is openly licensed third-party material.
Authors, licences and sources are recorded in `PHOTO-CREDITS.md`; keep the credits
with any redistribution. Prefer new imagery in the same palette and illustration
weight, and preserve the existing aspect ratios.
